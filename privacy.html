Held

Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Your worries stay on your phone. We never see them.

Held is a space for personal reflection, not professional care. It is made by the Held team (“we”). This policy explains exactly what Held keeps on your phone, the small amount of anonymous information it sends us, and what it never collects.

What stays on your phone

Everything you write in Held stays on your device. We never receive it. This includes:

  • the worries you set down, and how you rate them in the morning;
  • your morning-note setting;
  • a random install ID used to organise these records on your phone.

You can delete any worry, or clear them all, from “What you’ve set down.” Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. If your phone’s own backup is turned on (for example iCloud Backup or Google backup), your device may include Held’s data in that backup, which is stored in your own account under your settings.

What we collect: anonymous usage events

To understand whether Held is useful, the app sends a short list of anonymous events to our analytics provider, PostHog (hosted in the United States). Each event records only that something happened, never what you wrote:

EventWhenDetails sent
app_openedYou open Heldwhether it’s the first time
breathing_started / finished / skippedThe breathing exercisenone
worry_set_downYou set a worry downnone (not the text, not its length)
morning_openedThe morning check-in startswhether you tapped the note or opened the app
revisit_ratingYou rate a worry in the morningthe rating (heavier, same, lighter or faded), its position that morning, how many you’ve rated in total, and days since you installed Held
morning_finishedYou finish a morning check-inhow many worries it included
notifications_setYou turn the morning note on or offon or off, and whether notification permission was given
crisis_firedThe “if you need someone now” screen appears after writingnone; sent with a separate, temporary ID so it can’t be linked to your other events

Every event also includes:

  • a random analytics ID created when you install Held (not your name, email, phone number or any account), separate from the install ID on your phone;
  • the app version, your phone’s operating system and version, and device model;
  • your approximate country, which PostHog estimates from the IP address of your internet connection.

We keep analytics events for up to one year. We use them only to understand and improve Held.

What we never collect

  • anything you type, or anything derived from it;
  • your name, email, phone number, contacts or photos;
  • your precise location;
  • advertising identifiers. Held has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone for advertising.

Notifications

The morning note is scheduled on your phone. It does not pass through our servers, and it never includes what you wrote. You can turn it off in Held or in your phone’s settings.

Crisis resources

If you tap a crisis line or the “find a crisis line” link, your phone opens its own Phone, Messages or web browser. What happens there is between you and that service; Held doesn’t see it.

Accounts

Held has no accounts or sign-in.

Children

Held is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change what Held collects, we’ll update this page and its effective date before the new version of the app is released.
